The protocell's network

One continuous-time recurrent network of 24 neurons. Sixteen wall neurons sit on a closed ring; each senses the light at its place on the membrane and drives a cilium. Eight core neurons sit inside. Wall and brain are one structure, one weight matrix, one mask.

The weight matrix and its mask

Row i, column j holds the weight to neuron i from neuron j. Wall neurons are w0–w15, core neurons c0–c7. The mask allows 368 of the 576 entries: the ring's band, the two blocks of spokes, and the full core. Blank entries are fixed at zero.

One tick

Each tick (one second) the network takes one Euler step of Beer's equation, the wall neurons' outputs beat the cilia, and the cell moves with no momentum: its velocity is the summed pull, its turning the summed torque.

y ← y + (1/τ) · ( −y + W σ(y + β) + S )
Sᵢ = g · (Iᵢ + εᵢ) on wall neurons, 0 on core neurons
beatᵢ = σ(yᵢ + β_ring), the output of wall neuron i
v = v_max · (π/N) · Σ beatᵢ · uᵢ, uᵢ = direction of node i turned by α
ω = ω_max · (1/N) · Σ beatᵢ · sin α (+ rotational noise)
